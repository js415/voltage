Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about voltage stabilizers, air coolers, and our services

Voltage fluctuations are common in India and can damage your AC's compressor and electronics. A stabilizer keeps the voltage within a safe range (e.g. 220–240V), protecting your AC from low voltage, high voltage, and spikes, and can extend its life significantly.

80V–300V (wide range) stabilizers work even when voltage drops very low (e.g. during peak summer or in areas with weak supply). 130V–300V models are for areas where voltage rarely goes below 130V. Choose wide range if you face frequent low-voltage issues.

For a 1.5 ton AC, we recommend our TDI 4000, DV 480, WM 4000, or LS 4000 depending on your voltage conditions and budget. Check the working range (e.g. 80V–300V for low-voltage areas) and choose accordingly.

Yes. JSE Classic offers a replacement policy for stabilizers. Contact us for warranty details and replacement terms specific to your product.

Room size and humidity matter. For small rooms (up to 150 sq ft), 75–100 litre capacity (e.g. WINDSOR, MAXCOOL) works well. For larger rooms or commercial use, consider 110–150 litre models like CRYSTA, HAMMER, or MAHARAJA. Higher tank capacity means longer cooling without refill.

Honey comb is the cooling pad through which air passes. More surface area (e.g. 3-sided honey comb) gives better cooling and airflow. Our coolers use quality honey comb and oscillating louvers for even air distribution.
