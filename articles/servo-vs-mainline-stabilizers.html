Stabilizer Comparison

Servo vs Mainline Stabilizers - What's Right for You?

Servo and mainline stabilizers both protect equipment from voltage fluctuation, but they are used for different load sizes and different levels of voltage control.

Mainline stabilizers

Mainline stabilizers are commonly used for homes, shops, and selected appliance loads. They help protect multiple appliances connected through a broader circuit and are useful in daily domestic power conditions.

Servo stabilizers

Servo stabilizers are designed for higher precision and heavier loads. They are often used in showrooms, workshops, offices, factories, and commercial spaces where equipment needs more accurate voltage regulation.

How to decide

For household circuits and appliance protection, a mainline stabilizer may be the right choice. For sensitive equipment, industrial machines, or larger commercial loads, a servo stabilizer is usually more suitable.

Get load checked

Before choosing, calculate the connected load and understand the voltage problem at the site. This helps select the correct KVA rating and avoids under-sizing the stabilizer.
